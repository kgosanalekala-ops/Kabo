## Architecture domains
1. **IP CCTV endpoints:** fixed/PTZ/thermal cameras, lens and mounting specification, privacy zones.
2. **Electronic access control:** controllers, card readers, monitored doors, lawful credential handling, emergency egress.
3. **Protected infrastructure:** PoE switching, separate security network segments, resilient uplinks and appropriate identity controls.
4. **Recording and management:** VMS, NVR, capacity and retention modelling, role-based access, evidence export.
5. **Events and analytics:** ANPR and related capabilities only where suitable, lawful and validated for the use case.
6. **Control-room integration:** operator terminals, alarm queues, video walls, escalation procedures and environmental monitoring.

## Engineering discovery
Site layout, area coverage, camera counts, incident response responsibilities, PoE budget, storage retention and redundancy, integrations, cybersecurity, and the POPIA implications of personal-data processing.
